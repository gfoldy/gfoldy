# Career Baseline Audit (D14, Week 1, Session 5, 40 min)

Answer honestly in `job-search/baseline-answers.md`, then ask the coach to **"grade career"**. Each item is 0/1/2.

## J1. Resume
Paste your current resume (or link it).
- **2:** One page. ATS-friendly (no columns or tables, standard headings). At least 3 bullets with quantified outcomes ("cut deploy time from 40 to 8 min"). Skills match DevOps/platform job posts.
- **1:** Exists but is mostly a list of duties, not outcomes, or it's out of date.
- **0:** None, or older than 2 years.

## J2. GitHub portfolio
- **2:** 2+ pinned repos that show infra work (IaC, K8s manifests, CI), each with a README that explains *why* and *how*, plus a diagram or screenshot.
- **1:** Some relevant repos, but thin READMEs, or mostly tutorial clones.
- **0:** Nothing relevant pinned.

## J3. LinkedIn
- **2:** Headline names your target role. About section tells your story in 3-4 lines. Experience matches your resume. "Open to work" is set for recruiters.
- **1:** Exists but generic or stale.
- **0:** None.

## J4. Target roles
Write down 3 job titles you're aiming for. Paste 3 real job posts (links) for them, and for each one list the top 5 required skills.
- **2:** Done, and you can say which skills you already have and which you're missing.
- **1:** Titles only, no real posts looked at.
- **0:** "Anything in DevOps."

## J5. 90-second pitch
Record yourself (phone voice memo) answering "Tell me about yourself" for a junior platform engineer role. Write a transcript or bullets.
- **2:** Under 90 s. Past → present → why this role, with one concrete technical win. Mentions the homelab and what you're doing now.
- **1:** Rambles past 2 min, or has no concrete example.
- **0:** Skipped.

The coach writes your scores into `progress/diagnostic/manual-scores.csv` (rows J1-J5).
