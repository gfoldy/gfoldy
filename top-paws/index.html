The Top Paws bakery case

We bake every morning with simple, dog-safe ingredients. Nothing with chocolate, xylitol, raisins, onion or added sugar ever goes in the oven. Pick your treats, choose a pickup time, and pay when you come in.

Treats keep about 5 days in the fridge or 3 months in the freezer. If your dog has allergies, put it in the notes and we'll call you to go over ingredients.
