    Good to know

    When can I pick up?

    Saturdays only, at .

    When do orders close?

    Orders for each Saturday close . After that, you can still order for the following Saturday.

    What if I can't make it?

    Email us before the order cutoff and we'll move your order to another Saturday. Bread is baked to order, so we can't refund missed pickups after baking.

    Allergens?

    Everything is made in a home kitchen that handles wheat, dairy, eggs, seeds and nuts. Let us know about allergies in your order notes.

    How should I store my bread?

    Cut-side down on the counter for 2–3 days, or slice and freeze. Revive a day-old loaf with 5 minutes in a hot oven.
