# Job Search Track

Without a degree, the filter you need to get past is **"can this person prove it?"** The proof stack is:

1. **CKA** (Week 12). This is the most-recognized hands-on Kubernetes cert, and many entry-level platform job posts list it.
2. **Public capstone repo** with a strong README, diagrams, and a short demo video or GIF.
3. **Your existing DevOps/AWS experience**, rewritten as outcomes (numbers, scale, what got better).
4. **Interview reps.** Being able to explain your work out loud is the skill that gets you the offer.

| File | Purpose | Week |
|---|---|---|
| `baseline-audit.md` | Scored audit of where you stand now (D14) | 1 |
| `target-roles.md` | Role titles, the skills they ask for, and target companies | 1, refined in Week 6 |
| `resume.md` | Single source for your resume (markdown → PDF) | starts Week 2 or 6 (see `progress/plan.md`) |
| `applications.md` | Pipeline tracker | Week 9+ |
| `interview-bank.md` | Questions you've been asked + your best answers | grows weekly |

**Default schedule:** 1 h/week from Week 6. Applications start in Week 9, when the capstone is demo-able and the CKA is booked. If the diagnostic flags D14 ≤ 1, the track starts in Week 2.
