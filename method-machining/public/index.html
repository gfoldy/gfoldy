What we do

Capabilities

CNC Milling

3-axis and 4-axis milling for complex pockets, profiles, and features. Tight tolerances and clean finishes on every face.

CNC Turning

Shafts, bushings, fittings, and threaded components turned to spec with consistent concentricity and surface finish.

Prototyping

Quick-turn prototypes so you can test fit and function fast. We'll flag design issues before they become production problems.

Production Runs

Repeatable, documented processes for small-to-mid volume production with consistent quality from the first part to the last.

Repair & Custom Work

Reverse engineering, replacement parts, fixtures, and one-off custom pieces when you need something that doesn't exist yet.

Inspection

Every job is measured and verified against your print before it leaves the shop. Inspection reports available on request.

How we work

The Method

  1. 01

    Quote

    Send your drawings or CAD files. You'll get a clear, itemized quote — usually within one to two business days.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We review the design for manufacturability, choose tooling and workholding, and program the job.

  3. 03

    Machine

    Parts are cut with a documented, repeatable process so every piece matches the last.

  4. 04

    Inspect & Ship

    Critical dimensions are verified against your print, parts are deburred, packed, and delivered on schedule.

Who we are

About Method Machining

Method Machining LLC is a precision machine shop built on a simple idea: good parts come from a good process. Every job gets the same disciplined approach — careful planning, sharp tooling, and real inspection — whether it's a single prototype or a full production order.

We work directly with engineers, manufacturers, fabricators, and makers who need a shop that communicates clearly, quotes honestly, and delivers what was promised.

  • Straight answers. Clear quotes, real lead times, and a heads-up if anything changes.
  • Quality you can measure. Parts are checked against your print before they ship.
  • No job too small. Prototypes and one-offs get the same care as production.
  • Local & responsive. Talk to the person actually making your parts.
