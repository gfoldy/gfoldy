How we work

The Method

  1. 01

    Quote

    Send your drawings or CAD files. You'll get a clear, itemized quote — usually within one to two business days.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We review the design for manufacturability, choose tooling and workholding, and program the job.

  3. 03

    Machine

    Parts are cut with a documented, repeatable process so every piece matches the last.

  4. 04

    Inspect & Ship

    Critical dimensions are verified against your print, parts are deburred, packed, and delivered on schedule.

Who we are

About Method Machining

Method Machining LLC is a precision CNC milling shop built on a simple idea: good parts come from a good process. Every job gets the same disciplined approach — careful planning, sharp tooling, and real inspection — whether it's a short run or a high-volume production order.

We work directly with manufacturers, engineers, and purchasing teams who need a production partner that communicates clearly, quotes honestly, and delivers what was promised.

  • Straight answers. Clear quotes, real lead times, and a heads-up if anything changes.
  • Quality you can measure. Parts are checked against your print before they ship.
  • Every run matters. Low-volume orders get the same process and care as high-volume production.
  • Local & responsive. Talk to the person actually making your parts.
