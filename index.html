Hey, I'm Garrett

I help regular people get in the best shape of their lives.

I'm a personal trainer who fell in love with lifting because of how it made me feel — stronger, more confident, and more in control. Now my mission is simple: help you feel that too.

  • Programs tailored to your goals, level & equipment
  • Simple nutrition guidance you can actually stick to
  • Regular check-ins so you never train alone

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need a gym membership?

Nope. Your plan is built around whatever you have — a full gym, a home setup, or just some dumbbells. We work with your reality.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Absolutely. Most of my clients are everyday people who are new to training. I'll teach you the basics and build your confidence step by step.

Is this competitive bodybuilding coaching?

No. My focus is helping regular people get fit, strong and healthy in a sustainable way — no stage prep, no extreme dieting.

How do we communicate?

Everything runs through Instagram to start. You'll get regular check-ins and can message me with questions along the way.

How soon will I see results?

Many clients feel stronger and more energized within a few weeks. Visible changes build from there — the goal is progress that lasts, not a quick fix.

Ready to build a body you're proud of?

Spots are limited so I can give every client real attention. Send me a message and let's get you started.
