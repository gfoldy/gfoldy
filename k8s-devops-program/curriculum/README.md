| Block | Hours |
|---|---|

| Core labs | 4 |

## Why this order
- **CKA material comes first** (weeks 2-4), so the CKA domains get 10+ weeks of spaced practice before the exam.
- **Ecosystem tools come next** (weeks 5-9). They're what job posts ask for, and the capstone uses them.
- **Two dedicated prep weeks** (10-11) go into speed and exam technique, not new material.

## Adaptation
Weeks 2-12 are **scaffolds** until the diagnostic is scored. `adaptation-rules.md` defines exactly how your scores set each week's tier, move hours around, and trigger extra work.
